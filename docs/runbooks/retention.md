# Retention and privacy requests

- accounting ledger 10 years; accounting documents at least 5 years pending accountant confirmation
- order contact/address/support generally 5 years, then delete/de-identify unless dispute/hold
- incomplete checkout/reservation 30 days; invalid access token 7 days
- masked application/webhook ops events 90 days; normalized financial facts per accounting policy
- appointment completion +12 months
- case images closure +12 months unless dispute/hold
- newsletter until unsubscribe, then suppression HMAC/time only
- ordinary encrypted backup 30 days; escrow/configuration bundles remain through last dependent ciphertext/hold plus 90 days and never less than 10 years

Access, correction, deletion and restriction requests become audited cases. Legally retained data is restricted rather than falsely reported deleted.
